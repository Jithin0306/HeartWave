### LinkedIn (Engineering & Architecture Focus)
Building virtual camera software often means choosing between heavy 500MB+ Electron wrappers or sluggish cloud processing pipelines.

We built HeartWave with a completely different philosophy:
1. Native Rust Core (56 MB RAM consumption)
2. DirectShow & v4l2loopback direct kernel bridging for 0.0ms playout delay
3. On-device 21-point MediaPipe neural vision for real-time gesture reactions
4. Authentic Circle-of-Confusion optical bokeh shaders (f/1.4, f/2.8, f/4.0)

Seamlessly integrates with OBS Studio, Discord, Zoom, and Teams with zero capture cards required.
