# Brag Plan: HeartWave

## What is this app?
HeartWave is an AI-powered virtual camera studio that transforms any webcam or smartphone into an interactive broadcast camera with real-time DSLR optical bokeh, 21-point hand tracking with physical reactions, and 0ms reference clock sync in Discord, OBS, Zoom, and Teams.

## The angle
"Ditch the \$2,000 DSLR camera setup. HeartWave turns your everyday webcam into a cinematic broadcast studio with 100% local AI, zero cloud delay, and a 56 MB lightweight Rust core."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A cinematic camera viewfinder snaps onto screen with glowing reticle brackets `[ ]`, blinking red `● REC 1080p @ 60 FPS`, and the punchy opening claim: *"Your webcam doesn't need a \$2,000 upgrade. It needs HeartWave."*

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1: f/1.4 Prime Bokeh Optics**: Background smoothly melts from flat webcam blur into creamy, authentic optical depth-of-field as an aperture dial clicks to `f/1.4`.
- **Beat 2: 21-Point Gesture Reactions**: Creator makes a finger-heart gesture, 3D luminescent reaction particles bloom in mid-air with zero tracking latency.
- **Beat 3: Blazing Rust Performance**: Real-time HUD showing `56.2 MB RAM` and `0.0ms delay` in OBS, Discord, and Zoom.

## Outro / punchline
The HeartWave studio mark illuminates: *"Studio optics & expressive gesture reactions for everyday video. 100% Free & Open Source under MIT."*

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Webcam feeds into the studio camera viewfinder HUD.
2. **Action**: Real-time aperture dial turns on creamy f/1.4 bokeh + hand gesture triggers 3D reaction effects.
3. **Result**: Zero-latency broadcast stream piped instantly into Discord, OBS Studio, and Zoom.
